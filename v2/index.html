ASKED EVERY DAY.

No — and it never will ask for your PW login. PW has no public API, so the site tracks your backlog itself: live lectures are auto-added from your batch's weekly timetable, and everything else you add manually. Your credentials stay yours.

Open the site and the live sync adds every missed day's lectures automatically — about two per day, Mon to Sat. Then the kill plan simply re-divides the new total across your remaining days. No penalty screen, no guilt trip.

No account. Everything lives in your browser's localStorage on your device. The Backup button downloads a JSON file so you can move to another phone or recover after clearing browser data.

No. The teacher lock counts exactly one lecture per subject per day, from the teacher you follow — Manish Raj, SKC, Samapti Sinha or Vipin Sharma. The parallel batch is invisible to your backlog.

The leftover lectures roll into tomorrow and the target recalculates — remaining divided by days left. Your streak only breaks if you stay below your daily goal, not if one day goes soft.

Yes, it's built mobile-first from 375px up — the tracker is where you'll actually use it, between classes. You need internet for the first load; after that the app itself is a single file in your browser.
